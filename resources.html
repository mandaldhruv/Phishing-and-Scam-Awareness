Verified Cybersecurity Resources

A curated index of legitimate government portals, statutory regulators, and educational repositories dedicated to digital consumer protection.

Direct Contacts

Emergency Helplines (India)

1930

Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud

Direct line operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs to freeze fraudulent transactions during the initial Golden Hour.

112

National Emergency Service

All-in-one emergency response service connecting you with local police control rooms for criminal intimidation.

14448

RBI Financial Ombudsman

Reserve Bank helpline for unresolved banking grievances, unauthorized debits, and ombudsman complaints.

Authoritative Portals

Verified Official Portals and Guides

Government Portal India

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Official Ministry of Home Affairs platform enabling victims to lodge complaints regarding financial fraud, identity theft, and online harassment directly with state police cyber cells.

Official Address: https://cybercrime.gov.in
Visit Official Portal
Central Bank Reserve Bank of India

RBI Be(A)Ware Fraud Education

A comprehensive handbook published by the Reserve Bank of India outlining common modus operandi of financial fraudsters, precautions, and regulatory grievance redressal mechanisms.

Official Address: https://rbi.org.in
Visit RBI Website
Telecom Security DoT Portal

Sanchar Saathi Portal

Department of Telecommunications initiative allowing citizens to track and block lost or stolen mobile handsets (CEIR) and check the number of mobile connections registered against their name (TAFCOP).

Official Address: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in
Visit Sanchar Saathi
Technical Security CERT-In

Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)

National agency responding to cybersecurity incidents, publishing vulnerability notes, advisory warnings against active ransomware and phishing campaigns across India.

Official Address: https://www.cert-in.org.in
Visit CERT-In
International Education Consumer Protection

FTC Consumer Advice (US)

Clear, practical advice from the Federal Trade Commission detailing international phishing vectors, imposter scams, and methods for spotting deceptive callers.

Official Address: https://consumer.ftc.gov
Visit FTC Advice
Cyber Infrastructure CISA

CISA Cyber Hygiene Guidelines

Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency guides on foundational security habits: multi-factor authentication, strong passphrases, software updates, and recognizing phishing.

Official Address: https://www.cisa.gov
Visit CISA Portal
