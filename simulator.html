Interactive Scam Simulator

Practice identifying deceptive alerts in a safe, sandboxed environment. Review realistic simulated communications and learn to distinguish authentic notifications from fraudulent lures.

Educational Simulation Notice: All scenarios, phone numbers, sender IDs, and links displayed in this simulator are completely fictional simulations created strictly for cybersecurity awareness. No real transactions or external connections are made.

Evaluation Tips for Simulated Messages

  • Examine the Sender ID: Look for private 10-digit mobile numbers sending messages pretending to be official government or banking alerts.
  • Check the Domain Structure: Look past the brand name to see the actual host name. For example, in bank.com.fake-site.net, the true host is fake-site.net.
  • Note the Call to Action: Safe messages provide general status information; malicious messages push you toward third-party URLs or immediate payments.
