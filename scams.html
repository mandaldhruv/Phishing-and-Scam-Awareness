Directory of Prevalent Online Scams

A structured breakdown of common digital deception vectors, their warning signals, and how to verify legitimate communications.

Vector 01 High Volume

1. Email and SMS Phishing

Fraudulent messages mimicking banks, e-commerce retailers, or government departments. They include malicious links pointing to counterfeit login pages designed to harvest account credentials and payment card numbers.

Warning Sign: Urgent deadlines, typos in sender addresses, or strange domain extensions.
Read Detailed Phishing Guide
Vector 02 Financial

2. UPI and QR Code Frauds

Tricking victims into scanning a QR code or entering their UPI PIN under the false premise of receiving a refund, cash reward, or marketplace payment for goods listed on OLX or Facebook Marketplace.

Warning Sign: Being told that entering your PIN is necessary to "accept" or "credit" money.
Read UPI Safety Guide
Vector 03 Critical

3. Banking and OTP Scams

Callers impersonating bank officials claiming your credit card is blocked or reward points are expiring. They request the 6-digit OTP sent to your phone to authorize unauthorized financial debits.

Warning Sign: Any person on a phone call demanding the security code sent via SMS.
Read Banking Safety Guide
Vector 04 Search Trap

4. Fake Customer Care Numbers

Scammers place paid advertisements on search engines for airline, courier, or banking customer service. When victims call the sponsored number, scammers pretend to help while directing them to make payments or install apps.

Warning Sign: Customer support asking you to install remote software or transfer token verification fees.
Learn How to Verify Contacts
Vector 05 Social Engineering

5. Fake Work-From-Home Job Offers

Messages on WhatsApp and Telegram offering high daily wages (Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 8,000) for simple tasks like liking videos, subscribing to channels, or rating hotels. After paying small initial bonuses, scammers demand large deposits.

Warning Sign: Unsolicited job offers from unknown foreign numbers requiring advance "task security fees".
Try Scenario in Simulator
Vector 06 Technical Intrusion

6. Remote Screen Sharing Traps

Fraudsters instruct victims to install legitimate remote access tools like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport from Google Play Store or App Store. Once granted permission, they see your screen, view banking credentials, and capture incoming OTPs.

Warning Sign: Anyone asking you to share a 9-digit code or grant screen-mirroring permissions.
View Safety Checklist
Vector 07 Advance-Fee

7. Lottery and Lucky Draw Traps

Notifications claiming you have won a car, smartphone, or millions in a lucky draw (such as Kaun Banega Crorepati or brand anniversaries). To claim the prize, victims are asked to pay "customs fees", "file processing charges", or "GST".

Warning Sign: Winning a contest you never registered for, followed by demands for upfront clearance payments.
Test in Simulator
Vector 08 Logistics

8. Fake Delivery and Courier Scams

SMS alerts claiming a package cannot be delivered due to an incomplete address or unpaid Rs. 5 re-delivery fee. Clicking the link opens a counterfeit courier portal that steals credit card data and personal identity records.

Warning Sign: Demands for nominal re-delivery fees made via SMS links from 10-digit mobile numbers.
