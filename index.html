Fundamentals

Know the Warning Signs

Online fraudsters rely on predictable psychological pressure tactics. If an interaction contains any of these four elements, pause immediately.

Trigger 01

Artificial Urgency

Threats of immediate electricity disconnection, bank account suspension, or courier return within hours are designed to stop logical evaluation.

Trigger 02

Suspicious Links

Deceptive URLs containing typos, free domain extensions (.tk, .xyz), or misleading hyphens designed to impersonate trustworthy portals.

Trigger 03

Requests for OTP or PIN

Any person, caller, or form asking for your One-Time Password, ATM PIN, or UPI PIN. Remember: PIN is required only to send money, never to receive.

Trigger 04

Unrealistic Offers

Unsolicited part-time jobs offering Rs. 5,000 daily for liking videos, lottery wins for contests you never entered, or massive guaranteed crypto returns.

Essential Golden Rule

Three Things Legitimate Organizations Never Do

  • Never demand your OTP or UPI PIN: Bank officials and customer care will never ask for your one-time password or security PIN over phone, chat, or email.
  • Never require a PIN to receive funds: Entering your UPI PIN always debits your account. Receiving a payment or refund never requires authorization.
  • Never demand remote screen-sharing software: Genuine technical support will never instruct you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport to fix a payment.
