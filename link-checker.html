Technical Background

How Heuristic URL Detection Operates

In computer networking and security systems, heuristic inspection evaluates risk probability based on structural traits rather than static blacklists alone.

1. Hostname vs. Path

A standard URL is composed of: https://hostname/path. Attackers often place legitimate brand names in the subfolder or subdomain (such as sbi.portal.ru/login) where the actual domain being contacted is portal.ru.

2. IP Address Hostnames

Legitimate commercial organizations almost universally host consumer services on registered domain names with SSL certificates. Raw IP addresses (e.g. 192.0.2.1) in links are strong red flags.

3. Low-Cost Disposable TLDs

Free or ultra-cheap top-level domains (such as .tk, .ga, .cf, .xyz, .buzz) have historically experienced higher rates of disposable fraud campaigns because attackers can register thousands of domains cheaply.

4. Character Manipulation

The inclusion of special characters like the '@' symbol instructs standard web browsers to ignore everything preceding the symbol and resolve exclusively to whatever follows it, deceiving unwary users.
